Pricing

Pay only when
you break your pact.

There are no subscriptions, no monthly fees, and no hidden charges. You set the fine. You only pay if you break your own commitment.

🆓

App is Free to Download

Download FocusDetox on iOS or Android at no cost. Set up your pacts and start tracking immediately.

🤝

You Set the Fine

Choose your own accountability amount per pact — the minimum is $1 USD. The more you set, the stronger the commitment.

✅

Only Charged on Breach

If you honor your pact — you pay $0. The fine is charged only if you exceed your self-set time limit.

Keep your pact → $0 charged
Break your pact → your fine amount

It's that simple. You are only ever accountable to yourself. Payments are processed securely via Paddle, our merchant of record.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about
how you're charged.

Your card is only charged when you exceed the time limit on an active pact. We use Paddle to securely pre-authorize your payment method when a pact is created, but no funds are moved unless you breach your limit. You receive a notification when a breach is detected and before any charge is processed.
The minimum fine amount for any pact is $1.00 USD. There is no maximum — you can set fines as high as you believe necessary to motivate yourself. Keep in mind that high fines create strong incentives, and you are fully responsible for the amounts you choose to commit to.
Yes. You can pause or delete any pact from within the app at any time. However, if you have already breached a pact for that day (exceeded the limit), the fine for that breach is final and will be processed. Pausing or deleting a pact after a breach does not cancel the pending charge for that breach.
All fines are set and charged in USD (United States Dollars). Your bank or card provider may apply currency conversion fees if your account is in a different currency — this is controlled by your financial institution, not by FocusDetox.
All payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record. Paddle is PCI DSS compliant and handles all billing, invoicing, and receipts on our behalf. FocusDetox never stores your full card details. Paddle is a globally trusted payment infrastructure provider.
Because you set your own fine and agreed to the pact terms, fines charged for genuine limit breaches are non-refundable.
