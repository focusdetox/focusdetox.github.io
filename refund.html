1. Overview

FocusDetox operates on a self-accountability model: you create time-bounded focus sessions ("Detoxes") and, for Firm Detoxes, choose a Fine amount that you must pay if you decide to break the Detox before it ends. A Fine is only ever charged when you yourself initiate a break and complete the Paddle checkout. Because you explicitly define and agree to these terms — twice, once at Detox creation and again at the checkout — our refund policy reflects the voluntary nature of these commitments.

This Refund Policy applies to all charges processed through our payment processor, Paddle.com Market Limited ("Paddle"). All payments are processed by Paddle as our Merchant of Record. Refund requests related to billing errors by Paddle directly should also be submitted to Paddle at paddle.com.

Key principle: Fines you paid to break a Firm Detox are non-refundable. Charges resulting from technical errors on our side (e.g., duplicate charges) are eligible for full refunds. We evaluate all claims in good faith.

2. Non-Refundable Charges (Firm Detox Break Fees)

Fines paid to break a Firm Detox are non-refundable. When you create a Firm Detox you explicitly agree to: (1) the Detox window, (2) the Fine amount (minimum $1 USD per unit), and (3) that ending the Detox early requires paying that Fine. When you later choose to break the Detox, you re-confirm the Fine by completing the Paddle checkout. A charge that follows that two-step confirmation is the consequence of an agreement you entered into knowingly and voluntarily.

The following are specifically non-refundable:

  • Any Fine you paid in order to break a Firm Detox you created;
  • Fines where you regret the Fine amount you originally chose;
  • Fines where you wished, after the fact, that you had selected a Flex Detox instead of a Firm one;
  • Fines you paid because you wanted to use a blocked application during an active Detox.

This non-refund stance is fundamental to the accountability model — weakening it would undermine the product's core value for all users.

3. Eligible Refund Scenarios

We recognize that technical issues can occur. The following scenarios may qualify for a full refund:

3.1 Technical Malfunction (Our Side)

If you were charged due to a documented technical error on our side — for example, a payment recorded for a Firm Detox break that you never initiated — you may be eligible for a full refund of that charge. We will investigate using our server-side logs.

3.2 Duplicate Charges

If your payment method was charged more than once for a single Detox break due to a payment processing error, the duplicate charge(s) will be refunded in full.

3.3 Unauthorized Account Access

If you can demonstrate that a Detox was created and broken (and a Fine paid) as a result of unauthorized access to your account (not by you or anyone you permitted to use your account), we will review the claim and may issue a refund. You must report this within 7 days of the charge and provide supporting information.

3.4 Service Outage Affecting an Active Detox

If FocusDetox experienced a service outage that materially affected the operation of an active Detox or the break payment flow in a way that caused you to be charged in error, we will evaluate the claim on a case-by-case basis.

4. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, contact us within 7 calendar days of the disputed charge at:

Email: businessinquiryrn@gmail.com
Subject line: Refund Request — [your registered email] — [date of charge]

Your request must include:

  • Your registered account email address;
  • The date and amount of the charge you are disputing;
  • The name of the Detox and the apps involved;
  • A clear explanation of why you believe the charge was in error;
  • Any relevant screenshots or evidence supporting your claim.

Requests submitted after 7 days of the charge date will generally not be accepted, except in cases where the issue could not reasonably have been discovered within that window.

5. Refund Process & Timeline

Upon receipt of your refund request, we will:

  • Acknowledge your request within 2 business days;
  • Investigate using our server-side logs, Detox state history, and payment records — typically within 5–10 business days;
  • Notify you of our decision via email with a clear explanation;
  • Process approved refunds through Paddle within 5–10 business days of approval. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

The total timeline from request to refund credit is typically 7–21 days depending on your bank or card issuer's processing time. We have no control over your financial institution's processing speed once a refund is initiated.

6. EU Consumer Cancellation Rights

For users in the European Union, under the EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU), consumers generally have a 14-day "right of withdrawal" for digital services purchased online. However, this right does not apply where:

  • The digital content or service has already been delivered with the consumer's prior consent and acknowledgment that the right of withdrawal is thereby waived; or
  • The service has been fully performed.

When you create a Firm Detox you consent to the immediate performance of the Service (the Detox starts blocking applications either immediately or at the scheduled start time you chose, and the break-payment mechanism becomes operative) and acknowledge that the withdrawal right does not apply to Fines you later choose to pay in order to break a Detox. This acknowledgment is presented and confirmed within the Detox creation flow in the app.

If you believe this does not apply to your specific situation, please contact us and we will consider your case in accordance with applicable EU consumer law.

7. Israeli Consumer Law

For users in Israel, under the Israeli Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981 and its related regulations:

  • The Service's core offering — real-time accountability enforcement and the operation of the break-payment mechanism — is a digital service whose performance begins at the moment a Detox is created, and as such the statutory cancellation right does not apply to individual Fine charges once processed;
  • We are committed to fair dealing and will honor refund requests for charges that result from technical errors, as described in Section 3 above;
  • Nothing in this Policy overrides your mandatory rights as an Israeli consumer under applicable law.

8. Disputes & Chargebacks

We strongly encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Most issues can be resolved faster through our support process than through a formal dispute.

If you initiate a chargeback for a Fine that was correctly charged when you chose to break a Firm Detox, we reserve the right to:

  • Provide evidence of the Detox creation, the break action, and the completed checkout to your bank to contest the chargeback;
  • Suspend your account pending resolution of the dispute;
  • Terminate your account if a chargeback is found to be fraudulent or made in bad faith.

Chargebacks for legitimate technical errors that we failed to resolve after a good-faith request are entirely reasonable and we will not contest them.
