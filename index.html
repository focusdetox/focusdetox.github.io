How It Works

Three steps to a
mindful phone.

No subscriptions. No judgment. Just a simple pact between you and yourself.

01

Choose your distractions

Pick any app or website you want to use more mindfully — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, news sites, anything.

02

Set your limit & stake

Define a daily time limit (e.g., 30 minutes) and your fine amount — as low as $1. The higher the stake, the stronger the pact.

03

Honor it — or pay up

If you stay within your limit, you pay nothing. Only when you break your own pact does the fine kick in. Fully transparent.

04

Watch your habits shift

Real financial stakes rewire behavior fast. Most users report meaningful changes within the first week.
