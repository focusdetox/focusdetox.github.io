Delete your FocusDetox account

FocusDetox lets you request deletion of your account and associated data in two ways.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open FocusDetox.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and the data listed below are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email businessinquiryrn@gmail.com from the address tied to your account. We'll process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (name, email, user ID).
  • All authentication tokens and sessions.
  • All detoxes, recurring rules, and ladder progress.
  • Your settings and per-account preferences.
  • Records of any accountability fines, including which detox they were attached to.

What is retained, and for how long

  • Payment records — Paddle (our payment processor) retains transaction records for up to 7 years to meet tax and financial reporting obligations. These are held by Paddle, not FocusDetox.
  • Server logs — anonymized request logs may persist on our backend (Convex) for up to 30 days before being purged.
