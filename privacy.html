1. Who We Are

FocusDetox ("FocusDetox," "we," "us," or "our") is a digital wellbeing application operated by an individual or entity registered in Israel. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (iOS and Android) and website at focusdetox.github.io.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to all personal data collected by FocusDetox in connection with your use of our Service. It does not apply to data collected by third-party services we link to, such as Paddle (our payment processor), Apple App Store, or Google Play Store — please refer to their respective privacy policies.

We comply with:

  • The Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 and the Privacy Protection Regulations (Data Security), 5777-2017;
  • The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (Regulation 2016/679) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA);
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as applicable for California residents;
  • The UK GDPR for users in the United Kingdom.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: To operate, maintain, and deliver the Detox accountability system and all features of the app;
  • Payment processing: To enable Paddle-hosted checkout when you choose to break a Firm Detox, and to record the resulting payment;
  • Account management: To authenticate you via Google OAuth, manage your account, and communicate with you about your account;
  • Customer support: To respond to inquiries, resolve disputes, and investigate refund requests;
  • Security and fraud prevention: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, and fraudulent activity;
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or governmental requests.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, we do not sell your personal data to any third party, and we do not run third-party analytics SDKs in the application.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following categories of service providers, strictly as necessary to operate the Service:

6.1 Google (Authentication)

Sign-in is provided through Google OAuth. When you authenticate, Google shares your account email, Google account ID, and (if available) name with us so we can create and identify your FocusDetox account. We do not receive your Google password. Google's handling of your Google account data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

6.2 Paddle (Payment Processor & Merchant of Record)

Paddle.com Market Limited processes all Fine payments on our behalf as our Merchant of Record. When you choose to break a Firm Detox, you are redirected to a Paddle-hosted checkout page; your payment information is collected and stored by Paddle, not by FocusDetox. Paddle's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy at paddle.com/legal/privacy.

6.3 Convex (Backend & Hosting)

The application's backend (database, API, scheduled jobs, authentication state) is provided by Convex, operated by Convex, Inc. Convex hosts the data described in Section 3 on infrastructure located primarily in the United States (Convex's underlying infrastructure provider is Amazon Web Services). Convex acts as a data processor on our behalf and is bound by its own data processing terms; it is not permitted to use your data for its own purposes. Convex's privacy practices are described at convex.dev/legal/privacy.

6.4 GitHub Pages (Website & Checkout Page Hosting)

Our marketing website (focusdetox.github.io) and the static page that loads Paddle's checkout are hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect standard server logs (including your IP address) when you visit these pages. GitHub's privacy practices are described at github.com.

6.5 Distribution Platforms

The mobile application is distributed through the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. When you download or update the app, those platforms collect data subject to their own privacy policies. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying install metrics from these platforms.

6.6 Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal data if required to do so by law or in good faith that such action is necessary to: (a) comply with a legal obligation; (b) protect and defend our rights or property; (c) prevent fraud or illegal activity; (d) protect the safety of users or the public.

7. International Data Transfers

FocusDetox is operated from Israel. Israel has been granted an adequacy decision by the European Commission, meaning that data transferred from the EEA to Israel receives a level of protection equivalent to that provided within the EEA.

Several of our service providers are located in the United States — including Convex (backend hosting), Paddle (payment processing), Google (authentication and Play Store distribution), and GitHub (website and checkout-page hosting). For transfers from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to the United States, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, or equivalent mechanisms that the relevant provider maintains.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active and for up to 3 years after account closure, for legal and dispute resolution purposes;
  • Detox state and break data: Retained for up to 2 years to enable dispute resolution for payment-related matters;
  • Payment records: Retained for up to 7 years as required by applicable financial and tax regulations;
  • Support communications: Retained for up to 2 years after resolution;
  • Anonymized analytics: May be retained indefinitely as they do not constitute personal data.

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest, as provided by our infrastructure provider Convex;
  • Authentication delegated to Google OAuth — we do not store user passwords;
  • Server-side authorization checks ensuring each user can only access their own records;
  • Limiting administrative access to the data store to authorized personnel.

Your payment data is handled exclusively by Paddle, which maintains PCI DSS Level 1 compliance — the highest level of payment security. We never see, transmit, or store your card number or CVV.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to businessinquiryrn@gmail.com.

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete such information as quickly as possible.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at businessinquiryrn@gmail.com.

12. Cookies & Tracking

Our website (focusdetox.github.io) does not set any first-party cookies. Standard server logs are kept by GitHub Pages as our website host. The Paddle-hosted checkout page may set cookies that are strictly necessary for the operation of the checkout, as described in Paddle's Privacy Policy.

The mobile application does not use browser cookies and does not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Authentication state is stored locally on the device (in secure on-device storage) so you remain signed in between sessions.
