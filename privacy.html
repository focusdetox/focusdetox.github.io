1. Who We Are

FocusDetox ("FocusDetox," "we," "us," or "our") is a digital wellbeing application operated by an individual or entity registered in Israel. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (iOS and Android) and website at focusdetox.github.io.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to all personal data collected by FocusDetox in connection with your use of our Service. It does not apply to data collected by third-party services we link to, such as Paddle (our payment processor), Apple App Store, or Google Play Store — please refer to their respective privacy policies.

We comply with:

  • The Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 and the Privacy Protection Regulations (Data Security), 5777-2017;
  • The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (Regulation 2016/679) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA);
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as applicable for California residents;
  • The UK GDPR for users in the United Kingdom.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: To operate, maintain, and deliver the Pact accountability system and all features of the app;
  • Payment processing: To trigger and process fine charges via Paddle when a Pact breach is detected;
  • Account management: To authenticate you, manage your account, and communicate with you about your account;
  • Customer support: To respond to inquiries, resolve disputes, and investigate refund requests;
  • Security and fraud prevention: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, and fraudulent activity;
  • Product improvement: To understand how users interact with the app to improve features (using aggregated, anonymized analytics);
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or governmental requests.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with the following categories of third parties, strictly as necessary:

6.1 Paddle (Payment Processor)

Paddle.com Market Limited processes all payments on our behalf as our Merchant of Record. When you set up a Pact with a fine amount, your payment information is processed directly by Paddle. Paddle's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy at paddle.com/legal/privacy.

6.2 Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting

We use cloud hosting providers (such as Firebase, AWS, or similar) to store and process data. These providers operate under strict data processing agreements and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

6.3 Analytics

We may use anonymized, aggregated analytics services. No personally identifiable data is shared with analytics providers for advertising purposes.

6.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal data if required to do so by law or in good faith that such action is necessary to: (a) comply with a legal obligation; (b) protect and defend our rights or property; (c) prevent fraud or illegal activity; (d) protect the safety of users or the public.

7. International Data Transfers

FocusDetox is operated from Israel. Israel has been granted an adequacy decision by the European Commission, meaning your data transferred from the EEA to Israel receives a level of protection equivalent to that provided within the EEA.

Where we use service providers located outside Israel or the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or providers certified under equivalent frameworks.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active and for up to 3 years after account closure, for legal and dispute resolution purposes;
  • Pact and breach data: Retained for up to 2 years to enable dispute resolution for payment-related matters;
  • Payment records: Retained for up to 7 years as required by applicable financial and tax regulations;
  • Support communications: Retained for up to 2 years after resolution;
  • Anonymized analytics: May be retained indefinitely as they do not constitute personal data.

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest;
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt or equivalent);
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only;
  • Regular security assessments of our infrastructure.

Your payment data is handled exclusively by Paddle, which maintains PCI DSS Level 1 compliance — the highest level of payment security. We never store your full card number or CVV.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to businessinquiryrn@gmail.com.
