5. Pact System & Financial Commitments

5.1 What Is a Pact?

A "Pact" is a voluntary self-accountability commitment you create within the app. When you set a Pact, you:

  • Choose a specific application or website to limit;
  • Define a maximum daily usage duration (the "Limit");
  • Set a monetary fine amount (minimum USD $1.00) to be charged if you exceed the Limit on any given day (the "Fine").
Important: By creating a Pact, you are entering into a binding financial commitment. You authorize FocusDetox and its payment processor (Paddle) to charge your designated payment method the Fine amount each time you breach a Pact's daily Limit.

5.2 Fine Triggering

A Fine is triggered automatically and irrevocably when our systems detect that your usage of a designated application or website has exceeded the Limit set in your active Pact for that calendar day. The Fine will be charged to your payment method without requiring additional authorization at the time of the breach.

5.3 Minimum Fine Amount

The minimum Fine amount for any Pact is USD $1.00. There is no maximum. You accept full responsibility for the Fine amounts you choose to set.

5.4 Daily Reset

Pact Limits reset at midnight in your device's local time zone each calendar day. Each day is treated independently — a breach on one day does not affect the following day's fresh limit.

5.5 Pact Management

You may pause, modify, or delete a Pact at any time before a breach occurs for that day. Once a breach has been detected and a Fine has been triggered for a given day, the Fine for that day is final and non-reversible through pact management.

5.6 No Guarantee of Behavior Change

FocusDetox makes no representation that use of the Pact system will result in reduced screen time, improved wellbeing, or any specific behavioral outcome. Results vary by individual.
