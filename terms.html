5. Detox System & Financial Commitments

5.1 What Is a Detox?

A "Detox" is a voluntary, time-bounded focus session you create within the app. When you set a Detox, you:

  • Select one or more applications to be blocked on your device for the duration of the Detox;
  • Define a start time and an end time (the "Detox Window") — either by starting the Detox immediately for a chosen duration, scheduling it for a future window, or creating a recurring rule;
  • Choose a "Lock Level" — Flex, Firm, or Unbreakable — that governs how the Detox can be ended;
  • Optionally set a monetary fine amount (the "Fine") in whole USD units (minimum USD $1.00 per unit), which only applies to Firm Detoxes.

5.2 Lock Levels

Each Detox is created with one of three Lock Levels, which you select at creation time:

  • Flex. You may end the Detox at any time before it expires by completing an in-app friction step (e.g., a math gate). No Fine is charged. The Detox ends immediately when you choose to break it.
  • Firm. You may end the Detox before it expires only by paying the Fine you set at creation. Until the Fine is paid via our payment processor (Paddle), the Detox remains active and the selected applications remain blocked. If you do not break the Detox, no charge is ever made.
  • Unbreakable. The Detox cannot be ended early through the app. It will end only when its scheduled end time is reached. No Fine is associated with an Unbreakable Detox.
Important: When you create a Firm Detox with a Fine amount, you are entering into a binding financial commitment for that specific Detox. You authorize FocusDetox and its payment processor (Paddle) to charge your designated payment method the Fine amount if, and only if, you choose to break that Firm Detox before its scheduled end. If the Detox runs to completion, no charge is made.

5.3 How a Fine Is Triggered

A Fine is triggered solely by your own affirmative action of choosing to break an active Firm Detox while it is still running. Initiating a break opens a Paddle-hosted checkout for the Fine amount; the Detox remains active and the apps remain blocked until you successfully complete that payment. Closing or abandoning the checkout does not break the Detox and does not result in any charge. Fines are not triggered by passive measurement, by app usage, or by any time-of-day threshold.

5.4 Minimum and Pricing of the Fine

Fines are denominated in whole USD units, with a minimum of USD $1.00 per unit. You choose the number of units when creating a Detox or in your default settings. There is no maximum. You accept full responsibility for any Fine amount you choose to set.

5.5 Detox Management

Detox management depends on the state of the Detox:

  • Scheduled (not yet started): A Detox you have scheduled for a future time may be cancelled or deleted at any time before it becomes active. No charge can occur for a Detox that never becomes active.
  • Active: Once a Detox is active, it cannot be cancelled or modified. A Flex Detox may be ended by completing the in-app friction step. A Firm Detox may be ended only by paying the Fine. An Unbreakable Detox cannot be ended early.
  • Recurring rules: A recurring Detox rule (e.g., a Sleep or Family schedule) may be modified or deleted at any time; changes apply to future instances. An instance that is already active is governed by the rules above.

Once a break is initiated and a Fine is paid for a Firm Detox, that payment is final and non-refundable except as described in our Refund Policy.

5.6 No Guarantee of Behavior Change

FocusDetox makes no representation that use of the Detox system will result in reduced screen time, improved wellbeing, or any specific behavioral outcome. Results vary by individual.
